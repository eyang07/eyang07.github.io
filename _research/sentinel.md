SENTINEL is a framework for evaluating the physical safety of LLM-based embodied agents. As a research assistant in the IDEAS Lab (advised by Qi Zhu, supervised by Simon Zhan), I contributed safety-violation test scenarios — Python navigation tasks in AI2-THOR that simulate collisions and hazardous interactions.
